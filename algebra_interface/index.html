Examples: a+b=c+d, 3=3, 2+(3*(4*5+6)*(7+8)), [a+b]{c+d}, and (x). Brackets (), [], {} are all preserved. |, < and > are reserved. The name ab is one variable; _a _b is a product. Subtraction and function calls are not implemented yet.

Select a term or factor first.

Click to select; Ctrl/Cmd+click selects multiple operands. If adjacent siblings can be regrouped, click their thin blue outline to preview brackets, then click the preview to confirm (or Escape to cancel). Double-click a single term to toggle ( ). Alt+click selects a containing hitbox. For factorisation, first group the addends, then Ctrl/Cmd+click the matching leftmost or rightmost factor in each product. Drag a selected factor into the amber extraction zone outside the brackets, or choose Preview extraction. For distribution, drag a factor onto the + sign in an adjacent bracketed sum, or focus a factor and press D. Click any temporary amber factor to confirm; Escape or click elsewhere to cancel.
