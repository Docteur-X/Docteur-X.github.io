# Long-term product goals (not current functionality)

- Documents are worksheets containing multiple Mathematical Objects; windows are movable, resizable views, potentially several on one object, with independent focus and shared authoritative mathematics.
- Support relations beyond equality, compound statements with AND/OR, substitution with explicit justification, functions and assumptions, vectors and matrices with noncommutative operations and type constraints.
- A separate annotation layer supports circles, underbraces, labels and explanations anchored to mathematical identities, not embedded as algebraic rules.
- Preserve reproducible mathematical provenance, branching explorations, and optional teaching/explanation modes without taking strategic control away from students.
- Stay offline-first, low-resource-friendly, accessible to touch and keyboard users, with lazy capabilities and deferred inactive views where measured performance justifies them.

These are **design intentions**, not promises of existing implementation. Prior rationale: [archived clipboard/vision note](../06_Historical_Records/Archive/MATHEMATICAL_CLIPBOARD_V1_AND_LONG_TERM_VISION.md).
